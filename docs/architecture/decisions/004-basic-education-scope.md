# ADR 004 — Basic education beyond grades 3–6

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

The curriculum now covers Educação Infantil, Ensino Fundamental years 1–9, and Ensino Médio, and still excludes Ensino Superior.

Educação Infantil uses the three official age groups (bebês, crianças bem pequenas, crianças pequenas), not numbered school years. Objectives stay official learning objectives. A prerequisite between the same objective in successive age groups is a NaEscola proposal based on the BNCC table columns.

Ensino Médio skills are stored once, on `em-etapa`. The BNCC presents them without a série. The 1ª, 2ª, and 3ª séries are real school slots and do not receive an invented national skill list. Components without their own codes, such as Biologia or Filosofia, stay visible and point at the area that holds the official skills. Itinerários formativos are marked as local and are not enumerated.

Ensino Religioso remains matrícula facultativa in Ensino Fundamental and is an explicit omission in Educação Infantil and Ensino Médio. Língua Inglesa still starts at 6º ano. Computação remains the 2022 complement, including the pré-escola objectives and the Ensino Médio skills.

Grades 3–6 are regenerated by the same builder. Their skill texts, order, lesson metadata, and previous conceptual edges stay. New edges may be added; none of those edges are removed.

## Consequences

Coverage percentages use the larger topic set, so published-lesson percentages fall even though the twelve published lessons are unchanged. `curriculum/baseline-grades-3-6.json` locks the grades 3–6 contract. Schema validation, duplicate ids, broken references, and prerequisite cycles are checked by the catalog load, `pytest`, and `python -m app.automation.cli validate`.
